# Privacy-policy-for-Nitrogen-Notes
Privacy Policy for Nitrogen

Last Updated: 21-October-2026

Introduction
Nitrogen ("we," "us," or "our") is a notes and tasks application built with a privacy-first approach. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the Nitrogen app.

The core philosophy of Nitrogen is simple: your data belongs to you. We do not have servers, we do not track you, and we do not collect your personal information.

1. Information Collection and Use
Nitrogen does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage statistics, or analytics. Because the app operates entirely offline and locally on your device, we have no access to your notes, tasks, media files, or any other information you input into the app.

2. Device Permissions
To provide its features, Nitrogen may request certain permissions on your device. These permissions are strictly used for the functionality you initiate:

· Storage/Files: Required to save local backups of your notes to your device (in the Documents/Nitrogen folder) and to allow you to attach files to your notes.
· Camera and Microphone: Required only if you choose to capture photos, record videos, or record audio directly within the app.
· Photos/Videos: Required to allow you to attach existing media from your device to your notes.

These permissions are used solely to process your actions within the app. No media or data is uploaded to external servers.

3. Data Storage and Backups
All notes, tasks, and attached media are stored locally on your device. Nitrogen includes a feature to automatically save a copy of your data to your device's local storage. This ensures that your data is not lost if you uninstall the app. You have full control over this data and can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or manually deleting the backup folder.

4. Third-Party Services
Nitrogen contains no third-party tracking software, no advertising networks, and no analytics tools.

5. Children's Privacy
Because Nitrogen does not collect any personal information, it complies with regulations regarding children's privacy (such as COPPA). We do not knowingly collect or solicit data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the app or on the distribution page. Your continued use of Nitrogen after any changes signifies your acceptance of the updated policy.
